## The evaluation framework (unique IP — `/evaluate-driveways/`)

Three diagnostic questions:
> "Where's the water coming from, where's it going, and what's it doing to the driveway?"

Seven-point on-site checklist:
1. Where water enters the driveway
2. Whether water runs *with* the driveway or *across* it
3. Crown, cross-slope, and surface shape
4. Where water speeds up, slows down, or pools ("Fast water cuts. Slow water sinks.")
5. Ditches, swales, edges, and turnouts
6. Culverts and crossings
7. Material condition

Three outcome categories:
- **Surface Reconditioning** — drainage generally works, surface has degraded
- **Drainage-Corrective Grading** — water is actively contributing to the problem
- **Structural or Flow Intervention** — needs more than a grading pass; may be phased

Two entry paths: **Option A — Photo or Video Review** / **Option B — On-Site Evaluation**
