## 1. LandEx Earthworks — the one that actually matters
`landexearthworks.com` · 970-946-6905

**The threat.** This is the only local competitor already on a modern stack, and they are running
the exact playbook we'd otherwise be pioneering.

- **Next.js + Tailwind.** Same technical ceiling we're building toward. No "we're modern, they're
  WordPress" advantage available here.
- **Proof stack we cannot currently match:** "20+ years in mountain terrain", "500+ projects",
  "5.0 Google rating", "Licensed, bonded, insured", "75-mile service radius". DDG shows one
  testimonial and no numbers at all.
- **Aggressive SEO.** A 19-term keyword meta, keyword-rich titles, `/`-level positioning as
  "Durango & La Plata County's complete property enhancement specialists."
- Brand: green `#68A940` + blue `#426AAF`, Michroma display face, zero border-radius (hard,
  industrial). Competent, but corporate-generic — it could be any earthworks company in any state.

**Where they are beatable — and it's a real opening:** LandEx is a *generalist*. Rock walls, land
clearing, forestry mulching, septic, fire mitigation, building pads, roads, drainage — driveways
are one of nine services. They rank on breadth. They have no diagnostic framework, no
drainage-first thesis, no "here's why your driveway keeps failing" content.

> **The wedge: they sell capability, DDG sells diagnosis.** A homeowner whose driveway washes out
> every spring doesn't want a property-enhancement specialist, they want someone who can explain
> *why it keeps happening*. That is exactly what `/evaluate-driveways/` already does, and nobody
> in this market has an answer to it.

## 2. Accurate Backhoe & Excavation
`accuratebackhoe.com`

18+ years, Four Corners region, public **and** private road work. Genuine differentiator: on-site
**rock crushing**, recycling material to cut haul costs — a real, concrete, cost-based argument.
Squarespace build, orange `#E84C21` + teal `#046990`, Bebas Neue. Dated but credible.
Skews commercial/municipal; weak on the residential-homeowner emotional pitch.

## 3. Sunnyside Gravel & Excavation
`sunnysideexcavation.com`

**Established 1983** — by far the strongest longevity claim in the market, and they barely use it.
Broad general excavation: septic, land clearing, foundations, ponds, irrigation, retaining walls,
roads and driveways. Free site visits, licensed and insured. Duda-built, orange/green/cyan — three
competing accents, no discipline. Thin content, minimal SEO. A strong old business with a weak
website; if they ever fix the site they become a real problem.

## 4. Austin Asphalt & Gravel
`austinasphaltandgravel.com`

Primarily **asphalt**, gravel maintenance secondary. Wix. The OG site name still reads
**"My Site"** and the logo alt text is `"My Site"` — the template was never configured. `h1` is
10px. Effectively invisible in search. Not a digital threat; may still win on phone-book habit
and truck signage.

## 5. True Blue Landscape — dirt work division
`truebluelandscape.com/dirt-work`

Landscaping company with a dirt-work page bolted on. Driveway maintenance is a sideline, not a
specialty. Competes on convenience for existing landscape clients, not expertise.

## Benchmark — Bear Claw Land Services (Winter Park, CO)
`bearclawlandservices.com/services-1/driveways/`

Not a competitor (different market), but the closest analog: mountain Colorado, gravel driveways,
same weather problems. Worth studying because they do three things DDG doesn't:

1. **A published, transparent process** — online quote → site assessment → transparent pricing.
2. **An explicit "why gravel" argument** — affordable, fast to install, easy to maintain,
   environmentally sound. Sells the *category* before selling themselves.
3. **A named HOA offering.** DDG serves private/HOA roads but buries it. HOAs are high-value,
   multi-year, referral-generating accounts.

Also on Elementor, orange `#E4600C` — so their advantage is content strategy, not technology.

## What every single one of them fails to do

Across all six sites, **none** has:

- ❌ A diagnostic framework or any "why does this happen" education
- ❌ Before/During/After documentation of a real project with a stated scope
- ❌ Any interactive tool (cost estimator, symptom checker, quote calculator)
- ❌ A seasonal/timing argument (when to schedule, why spring vs. fall matters)
- ❌ Video
- ❌ Meaningful `LocalBusiness` / `Service` / `FAQPage` structured data
- ❌ A named, visible human owner as the brand
- ❌ Any real design ambition — five of six are template builds

**Where DDG already leads today:** the drainage-first thesis, the 7-point evaluation framework,
the three diagnostic categories, a documented Bayfield project with a real Before/During/After
photo set, a named owner with a face and a personality, and the most disciplined per-page metadata
in the market.

**Where DDG loses today:** proof (1 testimonial vs. "500+ projects · 5.0 rating"), no ratings, no
project count, no years-in-business, no license/insurance badges, an Elementor build with heavy
DOM duplication, three marketed services with no landing page, and a nav whose top item is
labeled "All Services" and contains "All Services".

## Strategic conclusion

The market is **wide open on expertise and closed on credibility.**

Every competitor sells *what they own* — equipment, years, service lists. Nobody sells
*understanding the problem*. DDG already has the best thinking in the market and the worst
presentation of it.

So the rebuild is not a design refresh. It is:

1. **Take the diagnostic IP that's already written and make it the product.** The evaluation
   framework becomes an interactive experience, not a wall of text.
2. **Close the credibility gap with real numbers**, gathered from Doug — projects completed, years
   operating, Google rating, license/insurance status, miles of driveway restored.
3. **Out-design all six by a wide margin.** Five are template builds; the sixth (LandEx) is
   competent-generic. A genuinely premium, editorially-designed site is an unmatched signal in
   this category — and in trades, the site *is* the proof of care.
